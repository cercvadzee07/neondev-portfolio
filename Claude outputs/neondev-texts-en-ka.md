# NEONDEV website — all texts (English + Georgian)

## Instruction for ChatGPT (copy this together with the texts)

Below are all the texts of my website in English (EN) and Georgian (KA). The Georgian texts were translated by an AI.
Please fix the **Georgian (KA) texts only**:

- grammar, spelling, punctuation and natural word order;
- make them sound natural for Georgian small-business owners (cafés, salons, clinics, tour agencies): friendly, simple, confident;
- the site talks to the client in the informal "შენ" form; keep that (one FAQ question uses "თქვენ" on purpose, keep it);
- keep prices (₾), numbers, brand names (NEONDEV, WhatsApp, Google, Facebook, Instagram, Viber, Messenger, TBC) and terms like SEO, QR, Linux, SaaS exactly as they are;
- keep each text about the same length, because it must fit the same place on the page;
- do NOT change the English texts and do NOT change the keys (like `hero.lead`).

Reply in the **same format**: the key, then the corrected KA text. Put ✏️ in front of every text you changed and add a short note of what you fixed. If a text is already correct, write "OK".

Notes:
- "—" means this text is not used in that language (the two versions have slightly different sections).
- `hero.lead` + `hero.decrypt` form one title: "თანამედროვე საიტი შენი ბიზნესისთვის — 5 დღეში".
- `pricing.meta` and `pricing.choose` show the result for all 3 packages, separated by "|".
